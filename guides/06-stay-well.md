# 6. Stay well

Nobody codes well on hour 30 with no sleep and only snacks.

- **Sleep.** Take at least a few hours on Saturday night. Rotate with your team so someone is always fresh.
- **Eat the meals.** Food is provided all weekend: come to the meal times, drink water, go easy on energy drinks.
- **Move.** Stand up and walk every hour or two. Take a real break outside the room at least once a day.
- **Ask for help.** Organizers and volunteers are around all weekend. If you or someone else feels unwell, unsafe or
  overwhelmed, tell an organizer straight away. In an emergency, call 911.
- **Be kind.** Everyone is tired by Sunday. Respect the
  [MLH Code of Conduct](https://github.com/MLH/mlh-policies/blob/main/code-of-conduct.md) standard that hackathons
  share: harassment of any kind is not tolerated.
- **It's fine to scale back.** Shipping one feature that works is a win. So is learning a new tool and not winning.
