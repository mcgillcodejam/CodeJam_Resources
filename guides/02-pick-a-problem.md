# 2. Pick a problem

Spend the first hour here, not in code. A small idea done well beats a big idea half done.

## CodeJam 16 challenges

Every project competes for Overall (1st to 3rd) and the popular vote. You can also aim at one or more challenges;
judges decide which challenge your project fits best, so make the fit obvious in your demo and README.

| Challenge | In one line |
| --- | --- |
| Fintech (Brim) | Brim's own challenge: Brim sets it, judges it and awards it. |
| Best Beginner Hack | Everyone on the team is at their first hackathon. |
| Most Sustainable | Cut emissions, a carbon footprint or AI energy use, or help conserve the climate. |
| Friday Night | An app you'd use going out on a Friday: it brings people together in person. |
| Health and Supporting Vulnerable People | Details at the opening ceremony. |
| Undo Button | Help people fix their mistakes. |
| Best Passive App | Gets you off your phone: it resolves things right away, or warns you at the right moment. |
| Best AI Solution | Details at the opening ceremony. |
| Best Pitch | Details at the opening ceremony. |

The opening ceremony has the full details and prizes.

## Score your ideas in five minutes

Give each idea 1 to 5 on each line and add them up. Build the highest score, not the coolest-sounding one.

| Question | 1 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Can you name **one real person** with this problem? | "People in general" | "My roommate, every week" |
| Can you show it working in **a 3-minute demo**? | Needs a week of setup | One clear flow, start to finish |
| Can your team **build the core in one day**? | New language, new framework, new hardware | Tools you already know |
| Does it **fit a challenge** clearly? | You'd have to explain the link | Judges see it in the first sentence |
| Is it **yours**? | A clone of an app everyone knows | A twist nobody else in the room will have |

## Write it down

Before anyone codes, agree on one sentence: "**[Project]** helps **[who]** **[do what]** by **[how]**." If you can't
write it, keep talking. That sentence becomes the top of your README and the first line of your pitch.
