# 7. After CodeJam

The weekend is worth more if you use it afterwards.

## Follow a team: the week after

Whether or not LastBus wins anything, the four of them leave with the same things: a public repo with their names on
the commits, a two-minute video of something they built that works, and three people they now know they can build
with.

On Monday Maya finishes the README while she still remembers how everything fits. On Tuesday Chloé posts the video
and tags the team. On Thursday Wen writes to the judge who asked about late buses, thanks him, and tells him she has
started on the live data. He answers the same day. None of that takes more than twenty minutes, and it is the
difference between a weekend they enjoyed and one they can point to.

Curious how the tools you used this weekend work underneath? [Stay curious](../extras/stay-curious.md) has a short
reading and watching list.

- **Keep the repo public and tidy.** Finish the README, add the demo video, keep the `codejam16` topic.
- **Put it on your resume** as a project with one line: what you built, with what, and one result.
  Example: "Built a transit-delay alert app in React and FastAPI with live STM data in 40 hours; won Best Passive
  App at CodeJam 16 (McGill)."
- **Post it.** A LinkedIn post with the video and your teammates tagged gets seen by the sponsors you met.
- **Follow up with people** you talked to (judges, mentors, sponsor reps) within a week, while they remember you.
- **Keep building** if you liked it. Plenty of startups and side projects began as a weekend hack.

Thank you for coming to CodeJam. See you next year.
