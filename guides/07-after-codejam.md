# 7. After CodeJam

The weekend is worth more if you use it afterwards.

- **Keep the repo public and tidy.** Finish the README, add the demo video, keep the `codejam16` topic.
- **Put it on your resume** as a project with one line: what you built, with what, and one result.
  Example: "Built a transit-delay alert app in React and FastAPI with live STM data in 40 hours; won Best Passive
  App at CodeJam 16 (McGill)."
- **Post it.** A LinkedIn post with the video and your teammates tagged gets seen by the sponsors you met.
- **Follow up with people** you talked to (judges, mentors, sponsor reps) within a week, while they remember you.
- **Keep building** if you liked it. Plenty of startups and side projects began as a weekend hack.

Thank you for coming to CodeJam. See you next year.
