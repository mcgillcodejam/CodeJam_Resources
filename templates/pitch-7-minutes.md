# 7-minute pitch template

5 minutes presenting, 2 minutes of questions. Invite the judges to try the demo around minute 3.
Copy this, fill in the blanks, and read it out loud with a timer.

---

**0:00 The problem (30 s)**
"Meet ______. Every ______, they ______. That costs them ______."

**0:30 What we built (30 s)**
"**[Project]** helps **[who]** **[do what]** by **[how]**. We built it for the **[challenge]** challenge."

**1:00 Demo (2 min)**
Show one flow, start to finish, as ______ would use it.
1. ______
2. ______
3. ______ (the moment it does something useful)

**3:00 Hand it over (1 min 15 s)**
"Want to try it? Here, ______." Give a judge the laptop or phone, or ask them what to try next.

**4:15 How it works and what's next (45 s)**
"Under the hood: ______ (frontend), ______ (backend or data), ______ (API or model)."
"The hardest part was ______. Next we'd ______."

**5:00 Questions (2 min)**
Prepared answers:
- How is it different from ______? ______
- What did you build this weekend versus use off the shelf? ______
- What would it take to make it real? ______
